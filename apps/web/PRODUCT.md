# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Teams planning and delivering work in shared workspaces and projects. Users need to create, organize, track, and complete work items without the project-management tool becoming a source of friction.

## Product Purpose

Plane is an open-source project-management product for tracking work items, running cycles, organizing modules and views, and understanding delivery progress. Success means teams can see what matters, act quickly, and keep project context in one place.

## Brand Personality

Clear, restrained, efficient. The interface should feel familiar, calm, and dependable while preserving Plane's established product language.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard styling, heavy or gratuitous color, unfamiliar controls, and visual flourishes that compete with the user's work. Do not introduce a separate visual language for an individual feature.

## Design Principles

- Keep the user's work and project data as the primary visual focus.
- Reuse established Plane components, tokens, spacing, and interaction patterns.
- Prefer compact, legible information hierarchy over decoration.
- Make states and actions immediately understandable from familiar product conventions.
- Add only the structure needed to complete the current workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional accessibility target is defined for the current work. Preserve the existing components' keyboard, theme, contrast, and reduced-motion behavior when extending the interface.
